BlurShield Options

Personal Info

Your emails and IPs are always masked on every active site, regardless of category toggles.

Custom Patterns

Add keywords or regex patterns to mask. Keywords are matched as exact text; regex patterns use JavaScript regex syntax.

Sites

Manage which sites have masking enabled.

Bypass List

Sites that are completely excluded from masking. The extension will not run on these sites at all.

Categories

Enable or disable pattern categories globally.

Preferences

Import / Export

Export your settings to share across machines, or import a previously exported config.
